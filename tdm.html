Therapeutic Drug Monitoring (TDM)

TDM is the measurement of drug concentrations in blood to individualise dosing for narrow therapeutic index drugs. Goal: maintain Css within the therapeutic window — above MEC (efficacy) and below MTC (toxicity).

Vancomycin

Target trough: 10–20 mg/L (serious infections). Trough <10 → treatment failure risk. Trough >20 → nephrotoxicity. Sample trough 30 min before 4th dose (steady state). AUC/MIC-guided dosing now preferred (target AUC/MIC 400–600).

Phenytoin

Target range: 10–20 mg/L (total). Nonlinear (MM) kinetics — small dose increases near saturation cause disproportionate Cp increases. Hypoalbuminaemia: measure free phenytoin (target 1–2 mg/L).

Digoxin

Target: 0.8–2.0 ng/mL (heart failure). Narrow window — toxicity (arrhythmias, nausea) above 2.5 ng/mL. Sample 6–12h post-dose when distribution is complete. Renal function critical — digoxin is renally cleared.

Other TDM Drugs

Aminoglycosides (gentamicin, tobramycin), cyclosporine, tacrolimus, lithium, theophylline, carbamazepine, valproate. All require individual dose adjustment based on measured concentrations.
