Multiple Dosing and Steady State

When a drug is administered repeatedly at fixed intervals (τ), it accumulates in the body until the rate of elimination equals the rate of input. This is steady state (Css).

Key Equations

Css,avg = F × D / (CL × τ). Time to reach 90% steady state ≈ 3.3 × t½. Accumulation factor R = 1 / (1 − e^(−Ke·τ)).

Clinical Significance

Drugs with long t½ take long to reach Css — loading dose needed. Drugs with short t½ reach Css quickly. Css,min (trough) must remain above MEC. Css,max (peak) must remain below MTC. Narrow therapeutic index drugs (digoxin, phenytoin, lithium) require TDM.
