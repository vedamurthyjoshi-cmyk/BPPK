Volume of Distribution (Vd)

Volume of Distribution (Vd) is a proportionality constant that relates the total amount of drug in the body to the plasma concentration. It is an apparent (not real) volume. Vd = Dose / Cp(0).

Interpretation

Small Vd (3–5 L): drug stays in plasma — highly protein-bound, hydrophilic (e.g. heparin, warfarin). Moderate Vd (15–40 L): distributes into body water. Large Vd (>100 L): extensive tissue distribution — lipophilic drugs (e.g. chloroquine Vd = 250–300 L/kg, amiodarone Vd = 60 L/kg).

Clinical Significance

Large Vd: dialysis is ineffective for overdose (drug is deep in tissues). Small Vd: responsive to dialysis. Vd determines the loading dose: DL = Vd × Css(target).
