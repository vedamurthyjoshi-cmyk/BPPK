One-Compartment Open Model — IV Bolus

After IV bolus injection, drug immediately enters the systemic circulation. The body is treated as a single compartment. Drug is eliminated by first order kinetics.

Key Equation

Cp = Cp(0) × e^(−Ke × t). Where Cp(0) = D/Vd, Ke = elimination rate constant = CL/Vd. Half-life t½ = 0.693/Ke. On a semi-log plot, this gives a straight line with slope = −Ke/2.303.

Parameter Calculation from IV Bolus Data

Plot Cp vs time on semi-log graph. Extrapolate line to t=0 → gives Cp(0) → Vd = Dose/Cp(0). Slope = −Ke/2.303 → Ke → t½ = 0.693/Ke. CL = Ke × Vd = Dose/AUC.
