Primary PK Parameters

Four fundamental parameters describe one-compartment IV bolus pharmacokinetics completely.

Ke — Elimination Rate Constant (h⁻¹)

First-order rate constant for drug elimination. Units: h⁻¹ (or min⁻¹). Slope of semi-log Cp-time plot = -Ke/2.303. Ke = CL/Vd = 0.693/t½.

t½ — Half-Life (h)

Time for Cp to fall by 50%. t½ = 0.693/Ke = 0.693 × Vd/CL. Independent of dose and concentration (first-order). Time to steady state ≈ 4-5 × t½.

Vd — Volume of Distribution (L)

Apparent volume relating total drug in body to plasma concentration. Vd = Dose/Cp₀ = CL/Ke. Not a real physiological volume.

CL — Clearance (L/h)

Volume of plasma completely cleared of drug per unit time. CL = Ke × Vd = Dose/AUC. Additive: CL(total) = CL(renal) + CL(hepatic) + CL(other).
