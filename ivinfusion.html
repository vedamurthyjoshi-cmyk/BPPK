One-Compartment IV Infusion

During IV infusion at a constant rate R₀, drug accumulates until rate of infusion equals rate of elimination. At steady state: Css = R₀ / CL = R₀ / (Ke × Vd).

Time to Reach Steady State

~4 × t½ to reach 94% of Css. ~5 × t½ for ~97% of Css. Pharmacokinetics, not infusion rate, determines time to Css. Increasing infusion rate increases Css level but does NOT shorten time to Css.

Loading Dose with Infusion

To rapidly achieve Css without waiting 4 × t½: give IV bolus loading dose DL = Css × Vd simultaneously with infusion. Used for: heparin, lidocaine, aminoglycosides in critical care.
