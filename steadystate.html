Steady State — Css,max, Css,min, and Fluctuation

Steady state (SS) is achieved when the amount eliminated during each dosing interval exactly equals the dose administered. Drug levels oscillate between Css,max (peak) and Css,min (trough) at SS.

Time to Steady State

Determined entirely by t½ — approximately 4-5 half-lives. Increasing dose or frequency does NOT shorten the time to reach SS, only changes the SS level.

Key SS Equations

Css,max = (D/Vd) / (1 - e^(-Ke×τ)). Css,min = Css,max × e^(-Ke×τ). Average Css = F×D/(CL×τ). Fluctuation = (Css,max - Css,min) / Css,avg.

Therapeutic Window

Css,max must stay below MTC (minimum toxic concentration). Css,min must stay above MEC (minimum effective concentration). Dosing interval and dose are chosen to keep both Css,max and Css,min within the therapeutic window.

TDM Sampling

Trough (Css,min): sample just before next dose. Peak (Css,max): at 1 h post-dose for oral, 30 min post-infusion for IV. Both must be at steady state (≥5 half-lives of dosing).
