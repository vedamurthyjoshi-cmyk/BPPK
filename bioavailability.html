Bioavailability

Bioavailability (F) is the fraction of administered drug that reaches the systemic circulation in unchanged form. IV administration has F = 100% by definition. Oral bioavailability is reduced by incomplete absorption and first-pass metabolism.

Measurement

Absolute F = (AUCoral / AUCIV) × (Div / Doral). Relative F compares two oral formulations. AUC is measured by the trapezoidal rule from plasma concentration-time data.

Key Parameters

Cmax — peak plasma concentration (related to intensity of effect). Tmax — time to reach Cmax (related to rate of absorption). AUC — total drug exposure (related to extent of absorption).

Factors Affecting Bioavailability

Dissolution rate, GI motility, food effects, first-pass metabolism, drug-drug interactions, formulation (immediate vs sustained release).
