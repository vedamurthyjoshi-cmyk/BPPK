Bioequivalence Studies

Two drug products are bioequivalent if they have the same bioavailability (rate and extent of absorption) within predefined statistical limits. BE studies are required for generic drug approval.

Study Design

Randomised, two-period, two-sequence crossover design. Single dose, fasting, healthy volunteers (n=12–24). Test (T) and Reference (R) products given with washout period ≥5 half-lives between periods.

Statistical Analysis

AUC and Cmax are log-transformed. 90% confidence interval of geometric mean T/R ratio must fall within 80–125% for both parameters. This is not a 20% tolerance — it is a statistical confidence requirement.

Parameters Compared

AUC (extent of absorption), Cmax (peak concentration — rate + extent), Tmax (indicative, not statistically tested). For modified-release: AUC, Cmax, Cmin or Cτ.
