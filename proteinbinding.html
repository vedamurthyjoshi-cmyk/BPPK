Plasma Protein Binding

Drugs in the bloodstream reversibly bind to plasma proteins, mainly albumin (for acidic drugs) and alpha-1-acid glycoprotein (for basic drugs). Only the unbound (free) drug is pharmacologically active, can cross membranes, and can be eliminated.

Key Parameters

Fraction unbound (fu) = Cfree / Ctotal. Highly bound drugs (fu < 0.1): warfarin (99% bound), phenytoin (90% bound). Low binding: lithium (0%), aminoglycosides (~10%).

Clinical Significance

Drug interactions at protein binding sites can displace one drug, transiently increasing its free concentration. Hypoalbuminaemia (liver disease, malnutrition) increases free drug fraction, potentially causing toxicity at normal doses. Volume of distribution is affected by protein binding.
