Nonlinear (Michaelis-Menten) Pharmacokinetics

Most drugs follow first order (linear) kinetics where clearance is constant. But some drugs saturate their metabolising enzymes at therapeutic doses — the rate of metabolism becomes capacity-limited. This is Michaelis-Menten or nonlinear pharmacokinetics.

Michaelis-Menten Equation

Rate of metabolism = (Vmax × Cp) / (Km + Cp). Vmax = maximum rate of metabolism. Km = Michaelis constant = Cp at which rate = Vmax/2. When Cp << Km: behaves like first order. When Cp >> Km: behaves like zero order — constant elimination rate regardless of dose.

Classic Example: Phenytoin

Small dose increments cause disproportionately large increases in Css. Half-life increases with dose. Requires individual TDM. Toxic levels are reached suddenly with small dose increases.
