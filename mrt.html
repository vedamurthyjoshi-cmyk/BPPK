Mean Residence Time (MRT) and Vdss

MRT is the average time a drug molecule spends in the body after a single dose. It is derived from statistical moment theory (SMT) — a model-independent approach.

Calculation

MRT = AUMC/AUC, where AUMC = Area Under the first Moment Curve = ∫₀^∞ t·Cp·dt. For IV bolus: MRT = 1/Ke = t½/0.693. For oral: MRT(oral) = MRT(IV) + MAT, where MAT = Mean Absorption Time = 1/Ka.

Vdss — Volume of Distribution at Steady State

Vdss = CL × MRT = Dose/AUC × MRT. Vdss is the most meaningful Vd parameter for two-compartment drugs because it is independent of the measurement time and the elimination process.

Clinical Significance

MRT is useful for comparing drug retention times across different routes and formulations. A longer MRT may indicate extensive tissue binding or slow absorption. MRT is used to design dosing intervals for modified-release formulations.
